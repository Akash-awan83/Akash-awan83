### [Self-Forecast Error Detects Shift, Not Errors](https://github.com/Akash-awan83/self-forecasting-unreliable-computation)

Can a network's error in forecasting its own activations reveal unreliable computation?

- 📈 **~9.7×** rise in forecast error under rotation shift, while softmax stayed **94–97%** confident
- 🎯 At catching individual mistakes: **0.56** vs softmax **0.84** AUROC
- 🧪 5 variants · 8 baselines · 16 shifts · 5 seeds · pure NumPy
